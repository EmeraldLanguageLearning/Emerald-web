Google Docs & Slides

Presentation add-on

Google Slides and PowerPoint: import each slide as a marked section of your script.

Script marks

  • *word* or *several words*: emphasis, held slightly longer
  • / short pause, // long pause
  • [Slide 3] on its own line: slide marker (added automatically for decks)
  • A blank line starts a new paragraph, with a longer breath

While prompting

  • Space play / pause · ↑ ↓ speed · ← → sentence · PgUp PgDn slide
  • + − text size · M mirror · F full screen · Home restart · drag or scroll to move, tap a word to jump
